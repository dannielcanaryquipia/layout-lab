    How to use Layout Lab

    Plan a slide

    1. Pick a slide of your pitch at the top: Cover, About us, Menu…
    2. Try the templates under the slide, or press Random layout until one gives you an idea.
    3. Drag blocks to move them. Drag the blue dot to resize. Everything snaps to the 12-column grid.
    4. Add blocks from the left panel: drag them in, or tap to add.
    5. Click a block and rename it with what you will really put there, like “Ube cup cutout”.

    Make it good

    1. Turn the design checks green: one focal point, room to breathe, edges on a few columns.
    2. Press Colors and type your own palette. Watch the contrast numbers.
    3. Type “(dark)” or “(accent)” in a card label to color that card.
    4. Open the Storyboard to see all your slides together. Print it or save it as a PDF.
    5. Download a PNG for your design brief, or Copy plan and rebuild it in Google Slides (View › Guides) or Canva.
